## Primary-file route and legal boundary

Official [recipe reference](https://docs.hytale.com/assets/item/recipes/), [release API](https://docs.hytale.com/api/) and [pre-release API](https://pre-release.docs.hytale.com/api/) support an authorized local parser; they are schemas, not a complete licensed recipe export. Separate recipes and embedded item recipes need coverage, resource groups, units, outputs, stations and inherited fields require explicit mapping. Existing importer/engine must reject unresolved semantics rather than invent quantities.

The official [Shared Source Agreement v2](https://hytale.com/shared-source-license), effective July21, is **not an unrestricted public-data licence**. Sections3.2,6.1,7.1,7.4–7.6 restrict standalone asset derivatives/distribution and access/storage; original work is treated separately. Whether a minimal factual recipe index falls within permitted independent original content is not settled by our reading. The [EULA](https://hytale.com/eula) also does not itself establish a public commercial export grant. Await the owner's already-sent studio clarification; do not acquire private-repository material or accept terms on the owner's behalf. No blanket claim that bare facts/IDs are copyrighted is made here.
